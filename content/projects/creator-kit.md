### The brief

More clicks on a video series and more watch-through on a paid social ad, on a budget that didn't stretch to a film crew.

### The move

Thumbnails with one face, one feeling and four words at most, tested at the size people actually see them. For the ad, an AI-generated base, then a hand-edited hook, captions and pacing until it read like a real creator.

### Delivered

- Thumbnail system with three reusable layouts
- Two 15-second vertical UGC-style ads
- Prompt library so the team can make more on-brand variants
