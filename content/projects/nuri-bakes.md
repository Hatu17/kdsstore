### The brief

A home bakery moving into its first shop needed to look established on day one, without losing the hand-made feel its regulars love.

### The move

A soft-serif wordmark with a single wheat sprig. It holds up at 40 px in an Instagram avatar and in gold foil on a black bag, where most of its customers will see it.

### Delivered

- Primary and stacked logo, plus a one-colour version for stamps
- Bag, tissue and sticker artwork
- Profile picture and story highlight covers
