### The brief

A new tea shop on a busy street had about three seconds to get a passer-by's attention.

### The move

Black and gold for warmth after dark, one large mark above the door, and a banner that states one thing: fresh chai, poured now. Everything else stays inside.

### Delivered

- Fascia sign and window vinyls
- Roll-up banner at 85 × 200 cm
- Menu board and loyalty card
